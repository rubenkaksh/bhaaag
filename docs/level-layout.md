# Level layout (L1), hillside redesign 2026-11-10

Supersedes the staircase street. One smooth hill, a straight fall line at x = 0, ten houses.

## Ground
- One planar ramp, 20 degrees, x ±34, z -104..26. The hero walks on it. Drawn surface equals the
  collider exactly inside |x| <= 34 (`src/adapters/render/terrain.ts`, pinned by `tests/content/hillside.test.ts`).
- Outside it the terrain blends into forested foothills, a valley floor below the last house and a
  far valley wall. Four invisible barrier walls (style 5, never drawn) keep the hero on the ramp.
- Generated by `tools/genLevel.mjs` into `content/levels/l1.json`. Never hand-edit.

## Houses (10 total)
- 8 route houses in 4 attached rows of 2 (house 2 of each row is a lower annex, `ROW_DROP` 2.6 m).
  Rows are 13 m of open hillside apart.
- 2 danger decoys (style 3) beside the lane at rows 1 and 3; row 1's decoy has a slippery pitched roof.
- Nothing else on the hill: no side villages, plots, fences or rescue stairs.

## Route
Spawn on house 0, anchors at the first house of rows 1..3 (route 0..2), decoy anchors 3..4.
A fallen hero restarts. Goal haystack sits past the last house's downhill edge.

## Scenery
Decor (flags, laundry, trees, terrace walls) in `decor/placeDecor.ts`, thinned per tier
(`setForestDensity`). Mountains: six heightfield rings in `render/backdrop.ts` (snow massif, three
hazy ridges, two forest ridges), reference `docs/reference/himalaya-valley-photo.jpg`. ADR 0008 holds budgets.

## Rope post
Tie post stands 2.6 m west of the spawn roof so the 20 degree span clears the roof corner by 0.5 m.

## Graph levels (ADR 0013 section 3)
Lobby entry "Valley Run" (`?mode=g:valley-1`, best time key `bhagdori.best.g.valley-1`). The level is a
graph file (`content/graphs/*.graph.json`: nodes are houses, edges are touch, zip, jump, tramp or
cushion hops) placed down a profile-shaped hillside by `src/content/graph/place.ts`. Zip source roofs
have no downhill parapet (it blocks the rope run-out). Frozen by `tests/content/graphFreeze.test.ts`;
bot-guarded by `tests/content/graphLevelBot.test.ts`. Drawing: `docs/level-graph-valley-1.md`.
