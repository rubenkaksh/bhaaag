## Hillside ground: soft landings and short jumps no longer kill (owner order after phone test)

- Owner: "ground is not hazard anymore on soft landings and short jumps but will result in really slow walking and some
  option to climb back to roofs."
- Rule (`src/core/sim.ts`, `hazardContact`): feet touching a hazard volume at impact speed <= `hazard.killImpact` (10 m/s,
  about a 5 m fall) put the hero on the volume's top as grounded `hill` ground. Above it, the kill plane, a vault and the
  rope with `rope.legHazardKill = 1` still kill (DEAD_HAZARD). `killImpact = 0` restores the old always-kill rule.
- On the hillside: walk capped at `hazard.groundWalkSpeed` (2 m/s), no sprint, no carried momentum, uphill steps taller than
  `hero.stepUp` act as walls. Landing on a solid, a mantle or the rope clears `hill`.
- Climb back (smallest option, no new button or geometry): hold the stick into a house wall for `hazard.climbHoldSec`
  (0.4 s); the hero rises at `hazard.climbSpeed` (2.5 m/s) to hand reach and mantles by the existing rule. Works for any ledge
  up to `hazard.climbMaxH` (14 m) with the usual head-room check. Jump or drop aborts the climb. Ground-level jump to a low
  ledge still uses the old grab.
- New keys (all five in `tuning.json` and `DEFAULT_TUNING`, kept equal by tests): `hazard.{killImpact, groundWalkSpeed,
  climbHoldSec, climbSpeed, climbMaxH}`. Remote overrides from PR #48 cover them automatically (the merge is driven by
  DEFAULT_TUNING's own shape).
- PRODUCT.md note: this softens the one-hit-death principle on the hillside (only hard falls kill there now). Ordered by the
  owner, so built; flag for the success test (finish a run under 2 min): slow walking costs time, climbing a tall wall costs
